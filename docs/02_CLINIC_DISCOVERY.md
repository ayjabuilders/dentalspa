## Open decisions to resolve with Dr Amina + designated reviewers
- Exact service catalog and durations by clinician/chair/location.
- Hollywood Smile as marketing service vs indication-based treatment plan; phases, alternative paths, true clinical gates and patient publication text.
- Staff roster per day, licensure/competencies, assistants as rotating roles, shift overlap, absence/delegation rules.
- Dental rooms/chairs and whether resources can host overlapping preparation/visit stages.
- Identity verification requirements for existing patient account linking.
- Fee structures, receipts, CNSS/insurance paperwork, payment approval, financial separation.
- Legacy Orthalis export availability, patient deduplication, parallel run and rollback procedure.
- Moroccan legal basis, privacy notice, recording consent requirements, CNDP filings/authorizations, international transfers, provider retention terms.
- Exact operational checklists and instruments/consumables by act; lab authorization and signed-off SOPs.
- Languages (French primary; Arabic support/RTL), patient accessibility, supported devices and network constraints.

## Discovery acceptance
A workflow is marked `validated` only after owner, authorized roles, mandatory safeguards, exceptions, data captured and patient-facing language are approved by the accountable clinic authority. Do not infer approval from a PDF sample.
